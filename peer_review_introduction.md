# Writing Assistant V3 – Peer Review Introduction

## Educational Purpose
This is a prototype writing coach designed for middle-school students (grades 5–7) working through iterative revision. The system provides AI-powered assessment that identifies writing issues across student-selected goals, offers scaffolded feedback (hints, vocabulary, sentence starters), and guides students toward voluntary completion with a reflective comparison of their original and final drafts.

## Why I Built This
I have two sons—my older one is in 5th grade and recently started writing short essays for his English homework. The traditional loop of "draft, teacher grade, revise" takes a long time and often doesn't provide enough scaffolding for students to understand what to improve or how to improve it. I built this app to give kids like him more immediate, structured support as they work through the revision process.

## Target Users
The interface is designed for middle-school writers working on argument, informational, or narrative pieces. You'll be testing it from that student perspective.

## How to Test
1. Go to https://writingassist-zhuang.streamlit.app
2. I'll provide you with sample student essays to work with
3. Work through a revision cycle: select 1-3 writing goals, review the feedback, edit the draft, and submit a revision
4. Try finishing with the reflection feature or continue for multiple rounds to see the full experience

⚠️ **Safety and privacy**: Use only the supplied synthetic sample essays. Do not submit real student writing, names, schools, or personal information. This demo sends input to an external AI service and is not production software for minors.

## What I'm Looking For
- **Feedback quality**: Are writing issues accurately identified? Are the scaffolds (hints, sentence starters) helpful and age-appropriate?
- **Usability**: Is the student workflow clear and intuitive? Do the interface elements make sense?
- **Pedagogical soundness**: Does the revision cycle support meaningful improvement? Is the reflection feature valuable?
- **Technical stability**: Any bugs, broken features, or confusing states?

Please note specific moments of confusion, unexpected behavior, or pedagogical concerns. Your feedback will directly inform refinements before further evaluation.
