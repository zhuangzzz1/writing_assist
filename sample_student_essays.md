## Essay 1: Should Students Have Homework? (Argument)

I think students should not have too much homework because it takes away from other important things. Homework can be good sometimes but too much is bad.

First of all, students need time to play and relax after school. We spend like 7 hours at school already learning stuff. When we get home we should have time to play outside or do sports or hang out with our family. My friend Sarah has so much homework every night that she can't even go to soccer practice anymore. That's not fair because exercise is important too.

Another reason is that homework causes stress. When you have a lot of homework you feel worried and anxious. Sometimes I stay up really late trying to finish everything and then I'm tired the next day in school. Which makes it harder to learn! So homework actually makes learning worse sometimes.

Some people say homework helps you practice what you learned. That's true but we could practice in school instead. Teachers could give us more time in class to work on stuff and help us if we get stuck. At home nobody can help you if you don't understand something.

Also homework takes away from family time. Instead of eating dinner together and talking my mom is always asking "did you finish your homework?" and I have to go do it. Families should spend time together not worrying about school work all the time.

In conclusion students should have less homework. Maybe just a little bit is okay but not hours and hours every night. We need time for other important things like playing, family, and sleeping. School is important but so is being a kid.

## Essay 2: My Trip to the Beach (Narrative)

Last summer my family went to the beach for vacation. It was really fun and exciting. I had never been to the ocean before.

When we first got there I ran straight to the water. The sand was really hot on my feet! The water was cold but felt good. The waves were big and kind of scary at first but then I got used to it. My little brother was afraid to go in so my dad had to carry him.

We built a sandcastle. It was huge! Me and my brother worked on it for like 2 hours. We made towers and a moat around it. We tried to put water in the moat but it kept sinking into the sand. It was still cool though. My mom took pictures of it.

The next day we went looking for shells. I found so many pretty shells! Some were white and some were pink and purple. One shell was really big and smooth. I also found a piece of sea glass that was green. My mom said it came from a broken bottle but the ocean made it smooth. I kept all my shells in a bucket to bring home.

One day we saw dolphins! They were swimming not too far from the beach. We could see there fins sticking up out of the water. Everyone on the beach was pointing and taking pictures. It was so cool. The dolphins jumped up out of the water and it was amazing.

On the last day I didn't want to leave. The beach was so fun and relaxing. We had ice cream and walked on the boardwalk. There was lots of stores and games. I got a t-shirt that says "Beach Life" on it.

I can't wait to go back to the beach next summer. It was the best vacation ever. I loved swimming in the ocean and finding shells and seeing the dolphins. Maybe next time I'll learn to surf!
